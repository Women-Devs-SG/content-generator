# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue for a security vulnerability. Report it privately through the repository's **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability**. If that option is not available, email the maintainers at [womendevssg@gmail.com](mailto:womendevssg@gmail.com).

Include a concise description, the affected version or commit, reproduction steps, and the potential impact. A maintainer will acknowledge your report and keep you updated on the fix.

## How the app handles data

The Content Generator runs entirely in the browser. Text you type, image URLs you enter, and exported images are not sent to or stored on any Women Devs SG server. Images you reference by URL are loaded directly from the site that hosts them.

Do not commit credentials, API keys, or personal information (such as real names, email addresses, or photos of people who have not agreed to it) in code, default template content, or screenshots.
